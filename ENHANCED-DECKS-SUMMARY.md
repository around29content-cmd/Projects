## Getting the `.pptx` files

The enhanced decks could **not** be committed to this repo as files: the cloud environment's
network policy blocks Moda's download host (`assets-cdn.moda.app`), so the exported files
cannot be pulled into the container. To download each as PowerPoint, either:
- open the Moda editor link above and use **File → Export → PowerPoint**, or
- use the per-deck export link provided in the chat (opens in a browser).

When you save each, name it `[Original_Name]_Enhanced.pptx` so the original stays available
for comparison.

> To enable committing the files directly in a future run, add `assets-cdn.moda.app` under
> **Allowed domains** in the cloud environment's Network access settings.

## What changed, per the standard enhancement pass

Applied to every deck (with prospect-specific verified content):
- **Slide 1 (cover):** amber accent rule + tighter title/subtitle spacing; where space allowed,
  a short strip of first-party credentials.
- **Slide 2 (understanding):** rebalanced the three columns (the thin "how customers reach you"
  column was deepened with the verified phone number and conversion-path detail); the "offer/proof"
  card carries only items verifiable on the prospect's own website (the slide footer asserts this).
- **Slide 3 (search opportunity):** a fuller, more balanced keyword-theme grid (verified service
  themes only — no unverified city claims), banner gap tightened.
- **Slide 4 (business-specific opportunity):** sharper, verified stat/offer cards.
- **Slide 5 (campaign approach):** deeper verified card bodies (themes, geography framed as
  "the cities you choose", landing-page rationale tied to verified site gaps, traffic quality).
- **Slide 6 (customer journey):** grounded step captions in the prospect's real assets.
- **Slide 7 (measurement):** "what we won't do" converted from a run-on to clean bullets; the empty
  lower-left filled with a non-promissory "THE GOAL" callout.
- **Slide 8 (implementation):** a concrete sub-line under each step; taller cards fill the space.
- **Slide 9 (next step):** a verified "if it's a fit" next-step panel + the Around29 tagline.

Every existing safeguard footnote was preserved verbatim ("Illustrative search themes… Not
keyword-volume research"; "A proposed approach for discussion… Not an audit of any existing
account"; "Everything on this slide comes from your own website…"; and the Zellers Medicare
non-affiliation / no-guaranteed-outcome disclaimers).

## Prospect-specific safeguards honored
- **AA Roofing:** free-estimate wording kept in its verified (roofing-page) context; no invented
  service cities; no "invisible on Google" framing.
- **American Tree:** the site's 24-hour-service, free-estimate and coupon wording kept and
  attributed to the site, with a "we'd confirm they're still current" note added; no invented
  locations; excavation left as a discovery question only; phone-first (unverified email not used).
- **Superior Coatings NW:** nothing from the unrelated Salt Lake City business used; the Verizon
  reference quoted once, explicitly as "no endorsement implied".
- **VIP Pest Solutions:** the only free-inspection mention (slide 4) carries the "owners who are
  not selling" condition explicitly; real-estate/selling inspections shown as the $95 service.
- **Zellers Group:** no government affiliation implied; no guaranteed coverage/eligibility/savings/
  enrollment; CMS-marketing-rules framing kept; only the pre-existing, verified enrollment dates used.
